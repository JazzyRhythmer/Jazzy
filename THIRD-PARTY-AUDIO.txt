JAZZY / BUNDLED THIRD-PARTY RECORDINGS

A / AMEN — “Amen, Brother”, The Winstons (1969).
Drums by Gregory C. Coleman. Amenity credits composition to Richard L. Spencer.
Source: https://github.com/ejtb76/amenity/tree/main/assets/third-party/amen

B / THINK — “Think (About It)”, Lyn Collins (1972).
Prepared from the Classic Break Pack recordings lyncollins_think_1.wav
and lyncollins_think_2.wav. The two-bar pattern uses drum intervals
from those recordings.

Both banks are two bars of mono PCM12 at 16 kHz and 160 BPM. Preparation
includes cropping, repitching, level adjustment and endpoint fades.
